Privacy Policy

Lookback · Effective July 3, 2026

Lookback collects no data. Everything the app does happens on your device, and your photos never leave your phone.

Who we are

Lookback is an "On This Day" photo-memories app for iOS, made by an independent developer. It resurfaces photos you took on today's date in past years. This policy explains what the app does — and, mostly, what it doesn't do — with your information.

What we collect: nothing

Lookback has no servers, no user accounts, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app makes no network requests containing your content or any information about you. Its App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected."

Your photos

Lookback asks for read access to your photo library so it can find photos taken on today's calendar date in previous years. This scanning happens entirely on your device using Apple's PhotoKit framework.

  • Nothing is uploaded. Photos are never transmitted anywhere, for any reason.
  • Limited access works. If you grant access to only selected photos, Lookback works with just those and never nags you to expand access.
  • We don't modify your library. The app requests read-only access. If you choose to delete a photo from within Lookback, the deletion is performed through Apple's own system confirmation dialog.
  • Sensitive-content filtering is on-device. Lookback can optionally blur or hide photos it detects as sensitive. This uses a machine-learning model bundled inside the app and runs entirely on your device — no photo or analysis result ever leaves your phone.
  • The widget is on-device too. If you use the Home Screen widget, today's photos are prepared and stored in the app's own private container on your device so the widget can display them. They are not sent anywhere.

Notifications

Lookback can send one optional daily reminder. This is a local notification scheduled on your device — no notification service or server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Purchases

Lookback Pro is an auto-renewable subscription processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles all payment information; Lookback never sees your name, payment details, or Apple ID. Apple's handling of that data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Tracking

Lookback does not track you across apps or websites, contains no advertising identifiers, and will never show you an App Tracking Transparency prompt — because there is nothing to ask about.

Children

Lookback does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — it doesn't collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If Lookback's privacy practices ever change (for example, if a future feature required a network service), this policy will be updated and the effective date above revised before that change ships. The current version will always be available at this address.
